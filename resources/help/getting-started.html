AgriPass handles registration, QR passes, check-in, CPD certificates and reports for PAA events. Staff work in two places:

  • Admin dashboard at {{APP_URL}}/admin/: events, registrations, payments, certificates, members and reports.
  • QR Scanner at {{APP_URL}}/scanner/: checking attendees in at the venue, on a phone, tablet or laptop.

Attendees never need an account. They register at {{APP_URL}}/, get their QR pass by email, and can sign in to My AgriPass at {{APP_URL}}/portal/ to see their events and certificates.

Signing in

  1. Open the admin dashboard or the scanner and sign in with the email and password from your Super Admin.
  2. The first time, you are asked to replace the temporary password with your own (at least 8 characters).
  3. To change it later, open your name at the top right and choose Change password.

After 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes, sign-in is paused for a few minutes. If you forgot your password, ask a Super Admin to reset it.

Roles

RoleWhat they can do
Gate StaffUse the QR scanner and view events and registrations (read only).
Regional OfficerEverything Gate Staff can do, plus create and run events, verify payments, issue certificates, manage members and download reports.
Super AdminEverything, plus creating and managing staff accounts.

The active event

One event at a time is the active event. It is the event shown on the public registration page and the default for every scanner. The green pill at the top of the dashboard shows which event is active. Admins can switch it from that pill or with Set as active event on an event's page.
