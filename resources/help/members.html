Everyone who registers for any PAA event is added to the Members directory, so each person has one record across all events. Returning attendees are matched by email address.

Finding a member

  • Search by name ("dela cruz" or "dela cruz, juan"), email, PRC license or PAA ID.
  • Filter by PAA membership status or chapter.
  • Click a member to see their details, every event they registered for, their attendance, payments and certificates, and their total CPD units.

Correcting details

  1. Open the member and click Edit member.
  2. Correct their name, email, mobile number, chapter, PRC license, PAA ID or membership status.
  3. Save. The change is recorded in the audit log with the old and new values.
  • Only staff can change a member's email or membership status. Members can change their other details themselves in My AgriPass.
  • Changing the email cancels any portal sign-in link sent to the old address.
  • Two members cannot share an email address or PAA ID. If AgriPass reports a duplicate, the person probably already has a record: search for it.
  • Certificates already issued keep the details printed on them. Correct details before issuing certificates.

Membership status

Active member, Inactive member or Non-member. New registrants start as Non-member; update the status from your membership records. It is used in the Reports breakdown and the members export.
