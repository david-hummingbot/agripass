Only Super Admins can manage staff accounts. Others: ask a Super Admin.

Adding a staff member

  1. Open Users in the sidebar and click Add user.
  2. Enter their full name and email, and choose a role (Gate Staff, Regional Officer or Super Admin). Give each person the lowest role they need.
  3. AgriPass shows a temporary password once. Use Copy and give it to them privately, not in a group chat.
  4. They must choose their own password the first time they sign in.

Forgotten passwords

Click Reset password on their row. A new temporary password is shown, and they must change it at their next sign-in.

When someone leaves or changes role

  • Click Deactivate to block their access immediately, including on scanners where they are already signed in. Their past scans and actions stay on record.
  • Reactivate restores access.
  • Role changes also take effect immediately.

Use one account per person. Do not share accounts: the check-in log and audit log record who did what.
