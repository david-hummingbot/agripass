The event's Registrations tab lists everyone who registered, with their chapter, PRC license, answers to your questions, payment status and how many sessions they attended. Use the filter to show only one payment status, and the search box to find someone by name, email, PRC number or chapter. Click a name to open their member record.

Verifying a payment

Online payment is not available yet, so attendees pay through their chapter, by bank deposit or GCash/Maya transfer to PAA, or at the registration desk.

  1. Find the attendee on the Registrations tab (filter by Pending).
  2. Click Payment on their row.
  3. Choose the status, enter the Reference no. (GCash/Maya reference or deposit slip number) and the Amount paid.
  4. Click Save payment. Their existing pass becomes valid immediately. No new email is needed.

Always record the reference number. It appears in the Attendance & CPD export, which makes reconciling with the bank or chapter much easier.

Scanners that are offline only see the change after their next Refresh roster.

Passes

Each registration gets one QR pass, emailed to the attendee and shown on screen after registering. The pass link works on any phone and can be printed. If someone registers twice with the same email, the second attempt is refused: one person, one pass per event.
