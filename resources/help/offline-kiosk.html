Working without internet

Many venues have weak signal. The scanner keeps a copy of the event roster on the device, so it can check people in offline and upload the scans later.

  1. Prepare while online. Open the scanner, choose the event and session, and wait for the roster to finish downloading. Do this at the office or hotel before travelling.
  2. Scan as normal. When the connection drops, the status at the top changes to Offline. Results still appear immediately.
  3. Reconnect. When the device is back online, queued scans upload automatically. The status shows Syncing, then All scans synced.

Registrations and payments made after the roster was downloaded are not on the device. Use Refresh roster in the menu whenever you have signal, especially after the registration desk verifies payments.

Do not sign out, clear the browser data or uninstall the browser while scans are waiting to upload. Queued scans live only on that device until they are synced.

  • If the status says Sign in to sync, your sign-in expired while offline. Sign in again and the queue uploads.
  • If some offline scans were rejected when uploading (for example a pass that was refunded), the scanner shows them under Review.
  • Scanning the same person on two offline devices is safe: the second scan is recorded as a duplicate, not a second attendance.

Self-service kiosk mode

Kiosk mode turns a tablet on a stand at the entrance into a self check-in station.

  1. Choose the event and session, then tap Self-service kiosk mode, or choose Switch to kiosk mode from the menu.
  2. Set an exit PIN of 4 to 8 digits. Staff need it to leave kiosk mode, so do not share it with attendees.
  3. The screen goes full screen and uses the front camera. Attendees hold up their QR code. Each result shows for about 2 seconds, then the kiosk is ready for the next person.
  4. To leave, tap the exit button in the corner and enter the PIN.

Station a staff member near the kiosk to help people whose pass shows Payment pending or Invalid pass.
