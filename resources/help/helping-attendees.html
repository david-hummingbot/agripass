"I lost my pass" or "I didn't get the email"

  1. Ask them to check their spam or promotions folder for an email from PAA AgriPass.
  2. On the registration page ({{APP_URL}}/), they can tap Email my pass again and enter the email they registered with.
  3. They can also sign in to My AgriPass at {{APP_URL}}/portal/, which lists all their passes.
  4. If they cannot get to their email at all, find them in the scanner with Can't scan? Find the attendee and check them in by name.

If they mistyped their email when registering, an admin can correct it from the Members page, then the attendee can request the pass again.

"My pass says payment pending"

Online payment is not available yet, so fees are paid through the PAA chapter or at the registration desk. Once an admin records the payment, the same pass becomes valid. The attendee does not need a new pass. Scanners that are offline see the change after Refresh roster.

Registering at the venue

Walk-ins register at {{APP_URL}}/ on their own phone or on a desk laptop, like anyone else. The pass appears on screen immediately after registering, so it can be scanned straight away. For paid events, an admin first records the payment.

My AgriPass (member portal)

Attendees sign in at {{APP_URL}}/portal/ with no password: they enter their email and receive a sign-in link that works once, within 30 minutes. In the portal they can:

  • see every event they registered for, with payment status, attendance and their QR pass;
  • download their CPD certificates, or see why a certificate was not issued;
  • update their name, mobile number, PRC license, PAA ID and chapter.

To change their email address, they need to ask PAA staff.

Checking a certificate

Every certificate has a QR code and a verification code. Anyone can scan it or enter the code at {{APP_URL}}/verify/ to see whether it is genuine and still valid.
