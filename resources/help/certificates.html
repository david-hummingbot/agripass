AgriPass issues a PDF Certificate of Attendance to each attendee who meets the event's CPD requirement. Each certificate carries the PAA signatory, the PRC accreditation number, the CPD units earned and a QR code for verification.

Who qualifies

An attendee is eligible when both are true:

  • their payment status is Paid or Waived, and
  • they checked in to at least the event's minimum attendance percentage of sessions (80% by default).

The event's Certificates tab shows every registrant, whether they are eligible, and the reason if not (for example "Attended 2 of 4 sessions; 80% required" or "Payment is Pending").

Before issuing

  1. Check the settings on the Certificates tab: CPD units, attendance requirement, PRC accreditation number and signatory. Use Edit to change them.
  2. Upload the signatory's signature under Signature & layout. A PNG with a transparent background looks best, up to 2 MB. Without one, a blank line is printed for signing by hand.
  3. Click Preview sample certificate to check the layout.
  4. Mark the event as Completed so attendance is final. Certificates cannot be issued before this.

Issuing

  1. Click Issue & email. AgriPass creates a certificate for every eligible attendee who does not have one yet, and emails it to them. Large events are processed in batches automatically; keep the page open until it finishes.
  2. To issue to one person, for example after correcting their attendance or payment, use Issue on their row.
  3. If an attendee did not receive the email, use the envelope button (Email again) on their row. Attendees can also download their certificate any time from My AgriPass or from their pass page.

Certificates show the name and PRC license as they were when issued. If a member's details are wrong, correct them on the Members page before issuing.

Revoking

If a certificate was issued in error, use the red Revoke button on its row and enter a reason. The certificate stays on record for audit, but verification shows it as revoked and the attendee can no longer download it.

Verification

Anyone, including the PRC or an employer, can scan the certificate's QR code or enter its code at {{APP_URL}}/verify/ to confirm it is genuine and still valid.
