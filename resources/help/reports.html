Each event's Reports tab shows live figures, so it is useful during the event as well as after it. It shows:

  • registered, attended and turnout, CPD eligible and certificates issued, total check-ins and how many came from offline scanners;
  • revenue collected, pending payments and the amount outstanding, waived and refunded registrations;
  • attendance per session, and arrivals per hour for each session (useful for planning gate staffing next time);
  • breakdowns by chapter, PRC license status and PAA membership;
  • totals for your registration questions, for example how many T-shirts of each size to order.

Session turnout is measured against registrants who are Paid or Waived, since unpaid registrants cannot check in.

Excel or CSV?

Choose Excel for anything going to the PRC. Excel removes leading zeros from license numbers when it opens a CSV file (0071234 becomes 71234), while the Excel file keeps them. Use CSV only for importing into other systems.

Exports contain personal information covered by the Data Privacy Act. Store them securely, share them only with people who need them, and delete local copies when you are done. Every download is recorded in the audit log.
