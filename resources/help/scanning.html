Each attendee has one QR pass for the whole event. Scan it once per session: someone who attends four sessions is scanned four times.

Before doors open

  1. On the phone or tablet, open {{APP_URL}}/scanner/ and sign in. Phones need the HTTPS address for the camera to work (see Troubleshooting).
  2. Under Scanner setup, choose the Event and the Session you are checking people into.
  3. Tap Start scanning and allow camera access when the browser asks.
  4. Wait until the roster finishes downloading. The scanner can then keep working if the internet drops.
  5. When the next session starts, open the menu (top right) and choose Change event or session. A scan always counts for the session shown at the top of the scanner.

Scanning

Point the camera at the attendee's QR code, either on their phone or printed. The screen shows the result with a sound and a colour:

ResultWhat it meansWhat to do
Checked inValid pass, paid or free, first scan for this session.Let them in.
Already checked inThis pass was already scanned for this session. The original time is shown.Usually a second scan by mistake. If someone else is using the pass, call a supervisor.
Payment pendingRegistered, but the fee has not been verified yet.Send them to the registration desk. Once an admin marks them paid, scan again.
Wrong eventThe pass is for a different PAA event.Check that the attendee is at the right event, and that the scanner is set to the right event.
Invalid passNot an AgriPass code, damaged, or altered.Find them by name instead (below), or send them to the registration desk.

If the code will not scan

  • In low light, tap the Flashlight button on the camera view (on phones that have one).
  • Ask the attendee to turn up their screen brightness.
  • Use Can't scan? Find the attendee: type part of their name, PRC license or chapter, then tap their name to check them in.
  • A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner also works: it types the pass code into the pass code box.
